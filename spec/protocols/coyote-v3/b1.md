# B1 response

4 bytes:

```text
byte 0: 0xB1
byte 1: sequence
byte 2: actual channel A intensity
byte 3: actual channel B intensity
```

- sequence 0: unsolicited device-side change;
- sequence 1..=15: response to a B0 intensity request;
- a mismatched sequence still carries actual intensity and must update observed state, but must not
  complete the pending request;
- actual intensity may be lower than the requested value because of the device soft limit.
